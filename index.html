Saishri Prabhakaran is an Indian actor who works in Tamil cinema. She began appearing in feature films in the early 2020s after working as a model. Her film credits include Rangoli, Indian 2 and Maria.

Career

Prabhakaran's early screen appearance was in Hey Sinamika (2022). Her feature-film debut is associated with Rangoli (2023), which brought her wider attention. She later appeared in the Tamil films Indian 2 (2024) and Maria.
