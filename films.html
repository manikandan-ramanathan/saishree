A defining performance · 2025

Maria

Saishri's first lead role follows a devout Catholic nun as repressed desires set off a profound spiritual and psychological crisis. Told through Maria's point of view, the film explores vulnerability, belief and the search for freedom.

Directed by Hari K. Sudhan and co-starring Pavel Navageethan, Maria screened at the Maharaja Film Festival, Coliseum International Film Festival and Kalakari Film Festival before its theatrical release on 3 October 2025. It is available on ShortFlix and Simply South.
